# Critique pass

Open `out/contact-<fmt>.png`, `out/phone-<fmt>.png` and any strips (every tile is stamped with its time), and look at them properly. You are a harsh
motion director reviewing someone else's cut, not the author defending it. Judge only what is visible;
if the plan says something happens but the frames don't show it, it doesn't happen.

Score 1–10:
- hook — does the first 2 s stop a thumb?
- readability at phone size — every word the viewer needs, at 360 px wide
- motion quality — springs settle, nothing slides linearly, no dead frames, no pops (strips)
- variety — something new every 2–4 s; no three shots in a row with the same move
- composition — one focal point per frame, balanced negative space, safe margins
- brand accuracy — real assets, right colours and type, nothing invented
- sound sync — hits on cuts and impacts (waveform vs cut times), silence only on purpose

Then list the 3 biggest problems with timestamps. Hunt specifically for: text overlapping other text
during a swap; things sliding instead of easing; labels or frames in the corners; a centred title on a
gradient; blurry scaled text; a beat where nothing happens; a stutter at a loop seam; numbers or text
that are not true (a spring overshooting a counter past its real value counts).

Fix those three, re-render only what you need to see again, make new sheets, write the new scores
in `docs/review_log.md`. Stop at all 8+ (at least 3 rounds); after 5 rounds, show the human.
